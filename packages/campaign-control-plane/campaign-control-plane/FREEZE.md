
# Frozen thesis

The portfolio is no longer a set of unrelated SaaS ideas. It is a laboratory for reliable autonomous construction.

The object being optimized is **verified progress per unit of human attention, time, tokens and money**, not maximum autonomy.

A project is a thesis plus a directed graph of behavioral claims. Each claim is only promotable when a deterministic validator can point to typed evidence that would be difficult or impossible to obtain without the underlying implementation actually working. Agents receive the next READY checkpoint, a budget, authority scope, evidence contract and reward. They may search, code, reuse GitHub, or spawn variants however they like; the gate is unchanged.

Multiple agents/seed variants may attack the same checkpoint. Their paths, costs, failures and receipts become comparable data. Seed0 learns routing/policy above the kernel. Repeated H-tasks and failure clusters become meta-bottlenecks for the human to solve once structurally rather than repeatedly intervene.

QP is the runtime boundary: cognition proposes, validators establish accepted truth, grants authorize consequential state changes. ATask records the run facts and bounded human decisions. The Campaign Control Plane turns product theses into the graph those systems operate over.
