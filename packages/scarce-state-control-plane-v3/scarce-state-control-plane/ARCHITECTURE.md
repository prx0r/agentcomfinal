## Human role

The intended bottleneck is explicit: human strategic judgement, physical/platform access, identity, trust and bounded authority. The human should not become a synchronous implementation coordinator.

## Three non-negotiable boundaries

1. **Simulation is not market proof.**
2. **Repo activity is not behavioral proof.**
3. **Agent confidence is not authority.**

## Scarcity hierarchy

Software/interface work is cheap manufacturing. External outcome generation, accounts, customers, permissions, physical observations, network state and trusted relationships are scarce resources. Campaign selection is therefore resource-aware.
