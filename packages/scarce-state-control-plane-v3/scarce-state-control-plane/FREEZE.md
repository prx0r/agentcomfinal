# v3 freeze — 2026-09-14

Frozen thesis:

1. Cognition/software/interfaces trend toward abundance.
2. Consequential external state remains scarce.
3. Seesaw searches for migrating scarcity, not merely software ideas.
4. ChatGPT apps, Etsy, markets and outreach are **experimental worlds** that provide different feedback qualities.
5. Internal simulation is useful but cannot prove demand, payment or scarce-state acquisition.
6. UNDERENGINEER chooses the shortest credible path to the next real information/moat event.
7. External accounts, stores, permissions, cash and human attention are explicit finite resources.
8. The human is a strategic/physical/authority exception handler, not the default project manager.
9. Successful mechanisms may replicate only from real outcome evidence.
10. BreadUp is dogfooded by this system itself: demand signals become product probes and normalized outcome data.
11. QP remains the authority/truth boundary; ATask remains execution state; Seed0 learns from trajectories.
12. Repo/code presence can never by itself promote a behavioral or economic claim.
